# Nexus Dental — Two-Month Trial Campaign

## Audience filter

Dental clinic owners, practice managers and lead dentists. The copy deliberately says “clinic” and “clinic team” so patients do not mistake this for a consumer dental offer.

## Meta primary text

Still managing appointments, patient records, treatment plans and billing in different places?

Nexus Dental gives your clinic one connected workspace—from the front desk to the treatment room, with a clearer portal for patients too.

For a limited time, selected dental clinics can try Nexus Dental free for two months.

Apply for a private walkthrough and see whether it fits your team.

## Meta headline

Try Nexus Dental Free for 2 Months

## Meta description

Limited onboarding slots for selected dental clinics. Terms apply.

## TikTok caption

One clinic. One connected workspace. Selected dental clinics can try Nexus Dental free for 2 months. Apply for a private walkthrough. #DentalClinic #PracticeManagement #DentalSoftware

## Lead form fields

1. Full name
2. Clinic name
3. Role at the clinic
4. City / country
5. Work phone or WhatsApp
6. Number of staff
7. Current way of managing the clinic
8. Biggest operational challenge

## Qualification note

The offer is application-based. Do not advertise “no card required,” “free setup,” unlimited support or a guaranteed onboarding date unless those terms are formally adopted.

## Landing destination

Use a dedicated campaign page or lead form until the public interactive demo route is deployed. Do not send paid traffic to a 404 page.
